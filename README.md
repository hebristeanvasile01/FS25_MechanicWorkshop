# FS25 Mechanic Workshop

A Farming Simulator 25 mod. Instead of repairing vehicles instantly, take them to the mechanic and wait for the job to be done.

## Features

- New **Send to mechanic** button in the workshop menu, next to Repair (click or **M**)
- The service takes 1 to 2 game days, depending on the damage
- While at the mechanic, the vehicle is gone: no TAB, no use. Attached dual wheels travel with it
- A notification tells you when the vehicle is ready, and it comes back where you left it
- **Quick repair** (click or **F**) is instant but costs double
- The service state is saved with the savegame
- Multiplayer supported
- Languages: English, German

Also available for Farming Simulator 22: [FS22_MechanicWorkshop](https://github.com/hebristeanvasile01/FS22_MechanicWorkshop)

## Settings

Service duration and the quick repair multiplier are at the top of `scripts/MechanicWorkshop.lua`.

## Installation

Download the mod from ModHub / KingMods, or zip the repository contents so that `modDesc.xml` is at the root of the archive, and name it `FS25_MechanicWorkshop.zip`. Put it in `Documents/My Games/FarmingSimulator2025/mods`.
